Paper Valley

Paper Valley is for people in hopes of reconnecting with the ones we never forgot. A brother you fell out with, a birth mother, a lost lover, a friend you lost track of thirty years ago.

Hang a lantern with their name on it and it stands on the trail where everyone walking past will read it. Somebody might know something.
