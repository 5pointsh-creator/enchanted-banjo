What the old folks said

Hunting & fishing by the moon

The old solunar tables: game and fish feed hardest when the moon is overhead or underfoot (the major times, about two hours) and when it rises or sets (the minor times, about an hour). Best of all in the days around the new and full moon.

Planting by the moon

From the old almanacs. Plant what grows above ground while the moon fills, roots while it shrinks - and best of all when the moon rides in a "fruitful" sign.
