5) SIMPLE SETUP GUIDE (phone)
----------------------------------------
STEP 1 - Create the account
   Open the Instagram app > Sign up > choose the username from section 1.

STEP 2 - Make it a free Business/Creator account (recommended)
   Profile > menu (top right) > Settings and privacy > Account type and
   tools > Switch to professional account > choose Creator (it's free).
   This gives you a clickable website link and simple stats.

STEP 3 - Profile picture
   Edit profile > Change photo > pick "banjospirits_profile_pic" from your
   saved photos.

STEP 4 - Name, bio, website
   Edit profile > paste the Name and Bio from section 2, and put
   banjospirits.com in the Website box > Done.

STEP 5 - Post the 9 images
   IMPORTANT: Instagram puts your NEWEST post in the top-left. So to make
   the grid line up 1 through 9, post them in REVERSE order:
   post 09 first, then 08, 07 ... and post 01 LAST.
   (If that feels fiddly, don't worry - just post them 01 to 09; it still
   looks lovely, only the order on the grid changes.)
   Paste the matching caption for each one.

STEP 6 - Add the Highlights (optional but nice)
   To add a Highlight you first need one Story. Post any image (even a post)
   to your Story. Then on your profile tap "New" under your bio >
   New highlight > select that story > Next > Edit cover > pick the matching
   cover image > name it (About, Trees, Stars, Play, Visit) > Done.
   Repeat for each.

That's it - your page is live and beautiful. Any trouble at all, just
message me and I'll walk you through it.
